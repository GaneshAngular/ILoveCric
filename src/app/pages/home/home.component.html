About I❤️Cric

Our mission is to revolutionize cricket management through technology

Founded in 2020 by a group of cricket enthusiasts and tech experts, I❤️Cric was born out of frustration with the lack of proper tools for amateur and semi-professional cricket teams to manage their operations.

We believe every cricket team, regardless of level, deserves access to professional-grade tools to help them perform at their best. Our platform is designed to be simple enough for casual players but powerful enough for professional coaches.

Today, we serve thousands of teams across 30+ countries, helping them focus more on the game and less on the paperwork.
